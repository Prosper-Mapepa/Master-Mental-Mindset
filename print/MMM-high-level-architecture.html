M³

System Architecture

Athlete Mindset Training Platform
1 · People

Athlete

Does a short weekly Mental Rep and check-in. Opens tools when needed.

Coach

Builds the season. Sees the team picture and who finished — not private answers.

Admin

Creates teams and codes. Publishes all content without a developer.

↓
2 · What they open

Athlete app

This week’s training, check-in, toolbox, progress.

Coach console

Season Builder, team pulse, completion, weekly coach tool.

Admin CMS

Teams, codes, modules, videos, tools, access dates.

↓
3 · Three products

Mental Reps

5–8 minutes each week. Learn, practice, apply.

Season Builder

Coach picks topics and order. One module unlocks each week.

Team Pulse

A 30-second check-in becomes team averages — never individual scores.

↓
4 · Privacy

Coach can see

Who completed training, team averages, and a suggested focus for the week.

Coach cannot see

Individual scores, reflections, or how any one athlete feels.
