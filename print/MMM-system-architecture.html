Athlete Mindset Training Platform · v1
1 · Actors & buying context
AthleteMiddle / high school. Owns login. Completes weekly Mental Reps and check-ins. Uses toolbox on demand.
CoachBuilds the season. Sees team pulse (aggregates) and who completed. Gets a weekly coach tool. Never sees private answers.
MMM AdminCreates schools, teams, codes, access windows. Owns the content CMS. Manages coaches and accounts.
School / programPurchases team access (outside the app in v1). Receives a unique team join code after provisioning.
Later · Athletic directorMulti-team oversight. Not in v1. Payments stay out of band until admin billing is added.

3 · Domain services — three cores plus supporting

Mental Reps

Weekly athlete session. Target 5–8 min, cap 10. Same shape every week.

Check-in Q Learn (video) Interact Apply Challenge

Season Builder

Coach sets week count, topics, module order. Weekly unlock. Optional recommended season.

Topics Sequence Unlock day Recommended path

Team Pulse

Weekly 1–5 ratings → team averages, trends, recommended coach focus. Never individual scores.

Energy Confidence Motivation Pressure Connection Enjoyment

Supporting

Mental Toolbox Coach tools Completion Progress Notifications Content CMS

Toolbox is on-demand. Coach tool matches this week’s module. Notifications: rep ready, check-in due, team % complete.

4 · Privacy gate
Minors · default private
Coach can see

Participation (who finished). Team Pulse averages and arrows. Completion %. Recommended coach resource.

Coach cannot see

Individual 1–5 scores. Reflection text. Toolbox use. Anything that identifies an athlete’s inner state.

Do not build in v1

Anonymous mental-health disclosure. No escalation path exists yet. Open-ended answers only if legally cleared.
